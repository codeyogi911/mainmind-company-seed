# Process: daily review

The one process worth having on day one. It runs every morning, it touches
nothing risky, and it is how an agent learns the shape of your business by
looking at it rather than by being told.

> This is a starting point, not a finished process. Delete the steps that do
> not apply and add the ones that do. A process you actually follow beats a
> thorough one you ignore.

## When

Every morning, before you start.

## What it does

1. **Read yesterday.** Orders, messages, payments — whatever systems this
   organization is connected to. Cite ids, never impressions.
2. **Reconcile the money.** Match what was taken against what arrived. A
   mismatch is reported as a fact with both numbers, not as an alarm.
3. **Answer what is already answered.** Anything covered by a standing rule in
   [AUTHORITY.md](../AUTHORITY.md) or a past ruling in `decisions/` is handled
   without asking, and the rule it acted under is named.
4. **Stop at everything else.** Anything reserved to the founder is prepared
   in full — the draft written, the numbers worked out, the cost of being
   wrong stated — and then held.
5. **Bring one brief.** What was handled, what needs the founder, and what is
   still unknown. Short enough to read standing up.

## What it must never do

- Send anything to a customer.
- Spend anything outside the standing limits.
- Guess a date, a price, or a cause. "I do not know yet" is a complete answer.

## What it leaves behind

- A record of the run in `records/`.
- A lesson in `lessons/` if something went wrong in a way that could recur.
- A proposal in `proposals/` if following this process was awkward, so the
  process can be changed rather than quietly ignored.
