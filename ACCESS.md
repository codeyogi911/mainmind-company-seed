# Knowledge access

Mainmind separates what a teammate may discover from what they are allowed to
do. This file activates repository-native knowledge compartments. A document
without an `access-scope` is visible only to the Founder.

## Compartments

- `core` — the common operating model, processes, roles, and non-sensitive records.
- `support` — customer conversations, cases, and support operations.
- `finance` — prices, margins, banking, books, tax, payroll, and financial records.
- `founder` — strategy, people matters, credentials, and anything reserved to the Founder.

The Founder grants compartments to each teammate in Mainmind's Team screen.
Grants control discovery across reads, search, path suggestions, counts, runs,
events, marks, decisions, and provenance. They do not grant operational authority;
`AUTHORITY.md` and the active Process still govern every external effect.

## Writing classes

- `ledger` — append a new, evidence-backed member through a typed Mainmind deposit.
- `conserved` — changes require a proposal and Founder approval.
- `ruled` — only the ruling flow may change it.
- `derived` — regenerate from its source; do not hand-edit it.
