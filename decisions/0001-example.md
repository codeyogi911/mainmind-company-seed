# Refunds under 50 do not need me

*An example, so the shape is obvious. Delete it once you have a real one, or
keep it if it happens to be true for you.*

## The question that was asked

A customer's jar arrived cracked. They sent a photo. The refund is 32.
Refund it without asking?

## What the founder said

> Yes. Under 50 with a photo, just do it. Do not make somebody post broken
> glass back to us — it costs more than the jar and it is a miserable thing to
> ask of a person.

Recorded 1 January 2026.

## What became true

- Refunds under 50, with a photo, are handled without asking.
- The customer is never asked to return a broken item.
- Above 50, or without a photo, it still comes to the founder.

## Why this is written down at all

Because the alternative is answering it again next week, and getting a
slightly different answer, and nobody knowing which one is the rule.
